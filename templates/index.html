    Net GEX by strike · ₹ crore per 1% movecalls puts spot zero gamma
    OI change since first snapshot · put − callcall OI building put OI building spot

    GEX (gamma exposure) estimates how much option dealers must buy or sell as the index moves 1% - Black-Scholes gamma × open interest × lot × spot² × 1%, calls counted positive and puts negative (the usual dealer-sign convention, which assumes dealers are long calls and short puts versus customers). Above the zero-gamma level moves tend to be dampened, below it amplified; the largest bars are the "call wall" and "put wall". Computed from this app's own 5-minute snapshots of the nearest expiry, so it fills in through the session. Educational only, not advice.

      Data preferences

      🤖 Autopilot

      When ON it ignores the preferences below and uses the tested rules per index: Nifty - Noise band, Camarilla, trend-day catcher (from ₹40,000 equity) and Fibonacci breakout (from ₹2 lakh); Bank Nifty - Noise band and Camarilla; Sensex - Noise band, trend-day catcher and Fibonacci. Always the 1-strike in-the-money option. Position size is worked out from your current equity (so it compounds): lots = 1% of equity ÷ risk per lot - 1.33% when the 5-min, 15-min and 1-hour charts agree with the trade, 0.67% when they don't (Nifty and Sensex, round 33), and on Nifty the AI judge raises or lowers that again (round 39) - with a minimum of 1 lot while one lot risks no more than 15% of equity, otherwise it skips the trade. A weekly loss limit (15% below ₹1 lakh, 4% above), counted on the system's own trades only, stops new trades. When OFF, everything works exactly as set below. It is rule-based, not a promise of profit - see the Research tab (round 20) for the small-account results. Press Save preferences below after switching it.
